# Decisions Log

Decisions the [agentic delivery strategy](../agentic-delivery-strategy.md) settled **without
escalation** — the evidence that the document is doing its job. One line per entry.

This is the counterweight to [`ambiguities.md`](ambiguities.md). A document that settles nothing is
not earning its maintenance cost; a document that settles everything is probably too vague to be
wrong. The ratio between the two logs is the quality signal, not either one alone.

Entries are also the sampling frame for the agreement rate: how often an agent's conclusion matches
what the operator would have concluded. That definition is owned by `#66` and is not yet written.
