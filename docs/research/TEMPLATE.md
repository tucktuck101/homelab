# Research: <Title>

## Question

The question this research exists to answer, stated as it was asked. If the question changed during the work, say so and state both.

## Why It Was Asked

What decision or work depends on the answer, and what the cost of getting it wrong would be. One short paragraph — the detail lives in the commissioning issue.

## Scope

**Investigated:** what was actually examined.

**Not investigated:** what was deliberately left out, and why. An unstated boundary reads as missing evidence rather than a decision.

**Timebox:** what was allowed, and whether the work stopped because it finished or because time ran out.

## Findings

The evidence, organised by sub-question rather than by source. Each finding states what was found and where it came from.

Distinguish clearly between:

* what a source **states**
* what the evidence **shows** when sources are compared
* what the author **infers** — marked as inference, not reported as fact

Contradictions between sources are findings in their own right. Record them rather than picking a winner silently.

## Recommendation

The answer, and the reasoning that supports it.

Where the recommendation departs from what the sources suggest, say so and justify it against the constraints in [`../vision.md`](../vision.md) — solo operator, real hardware, public by default, agents doing delivery.

## Confidence and Gaps

How far this can be relied on, and what would change the answer. Name anything that was assumed rather than established.

## Consequences

What follows if the recommendation is adopted — documents to write, decisions to record, work to raise. Link the issues created.
